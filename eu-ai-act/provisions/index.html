Ghosted rows have a positional hash and no page. They are listed rather than omitted because the hash is already stable and already citable — the address exists before the page does, which is the point of deriving it from the position rather than from the content.

How a positional hash is derived

positional_hash = sha256("<instrument-slug>/<alias>")[:16]

sha256("eu-ai-act/art-026-para-05")[:16] = d8fc6df65f292bf3
