The network, and what this site does not own

Seven sites carry related work between them. Boundaries matter more here than on most of them, because “standards” is a word broad enough to swallow half the network — so this page states what belongs to this site, what belongs elsewhere, and where the one live conflict is.

The rule these boundaries follow

Cross-link page to page, not domain to domain.

A link that says “see risks.sgit.ai” is an instruction to go and search. A link that says this specific argument, on that specific page is a join. The network's own access review found three sites each holding a third of one answer and unjoined at the page level — so every cross-reference on this site points at a page.

Two things this site deliberately does not do

Not here

Rebuild the risk apparatus

The grounding ladder, RAMM, accepted-vs-acceptable and the plug profile are substantial and they are a different site. This site uses the ladder and does not re-explain it. The boundary →

Not here

Own the vault catalogue

Twelve vaults are published across the estate and one of them indexes the others. This site's vault page mirrors the conventions and links back rather than inventing a second index that will drift.
